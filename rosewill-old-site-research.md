## Reusable Brand Language

### Intro / Hero

The Rosewill Loving Arms Covenant House (RLACH) will be a safe place for females ages 13-20 during their most vulnerable times in their lives. We make a promise to protect the females with our lives so that they can overcome trauma they have experienced, allow them to heal, be successful, educated and confident young women.

### Power Statement

Rosewill is designed to be a transitional home for sexually abused, sexual assault and sex exploited females. Here they will have a unique home setting to allow the females to feel they have this safe haven to focus solely on their recovery and future.

### Mission

Rosewill Loving Arms will provide a safe haven with therapeutic transitional housing, to those impacted by sexual abuse, sexual assault and sex trafficking.

### Vision

Rosewill Loving Arms Covenant House (RLACH), shall assist female youth with their healing journey who have experienced sexual trauma, throughout the states of SC, NC and GA.

### About / Positioning

Our goal is to provide transformative care for young girls overcoming sexual trauma, fostering healing and empowerment in a secure environment.

The care team empowers young women to heal from trauma and build confident, successful futures through personalized support.

## How It Works

1. Evaluation & Diagnosis
Each of the young ladies is evaluated and assigned by a counselor or agency.

2. Weekly Care Plan
Each girl has a custom care plan designed for them based on their evaluation results. No two traumas are exactly alike and should be treated with the utmost care.

3. Start of the Care Journey
Before girls can be assigned to the home they must pass a physical and psychological evaluation. Everyone is not automatically admitted and we want our clients to be successful from the start.

## Differentiators

- Trauma Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT) services.
- Same/next-day appointments: available 7 days per week and generally within 24 hours.
- On-site services: most services are provided at the home so girls do not have to be exposed to the public.
- Three-state coverage: South Carolina, North Carolina, and Georgia.
- In certain situations, willing to relocate women who need to be transferred from another state or agency due to abuse.

### Full Founder Bio

Wileen J. Stokes was born and raised in Bronx, NY. She was educated in New York City Public School system. She went on to receive her undergraduate degree, Bachelor of Science (BS) Degree in Business Administration from York College, City University of New York and pursued higher education. In May 2012 she completed a Masters of Arts (MA) Degree in Community Counseling at Webster University in Greenville, SC.

Wileen relocated to South Carolina in February 2006, where she presently resides.

Wileen volunteered her time and served various organizations such as Volunteers of America Greater New York. Also while residing in South Carolina, she volunteered her time and talents to Department of Juvenile Justice and Family Court as an Auxiliary Probation Officer. Her home in South Carolina was granted a license to conduct a Child Placing Agency Foster Home for two years, where she provided therapeutic services to children from ages six to twenty-one years of age.

She continues to serve her community in Gray Court, SC where she provides events entertainment for children.

When relocating to the Greenville, SC area, here is where she rediscovered the vision GOD placed in her to give back to female youth who have experienced sexual trauma, specifically sexual abuse. In 2009, Rosewill Loving Arms Covenant House was established as a 501(c) 3 Non-profit Organization. The organization was founded to serve not only the state of South Carolina, also North Carolina and the state of Georgia.

## Services / Programs

### 24/7 Transitional Care

Rosewill is designed to be a transitional home where the females will be residents from 6-18 months. The home will provide 24/7 care for the females to include counseling, transportation for education, job placement and medical attention when needed.

### Youth Workshops

- Bullying Prevention: Tips to prevent bullying are discussed and the girls are allowed to interact with the speakers and one another to prepare them for the inevitable bully.
- Healthy Relationships: The girls have the opportunity to discuss and engage in dialogue regarding dating violence, acquaintance rape, child sexual abuse and stalking. The sessions end with a summary of what healthy relationships look like.
- Proper Hygiene: Information is shared and discussed concerning routine personal care such as washing face, brushing teeth, grooming hair, body odor, athlete's foot, and other personal hygiene topics. Information is provided to introduce the girls to the importance of appearances and mannerisms.
- Life Skills: We assist the young ladies with life skills to include finances, cooking, household chores, etc.
- Fundraisers: To provide funds for additional costs which may arise while caring for females.

### Additional Service Cards

- 24/7 Emergency Placement and Care: Rosewill Loving Arms Covenant House provides a therapeutic, transitional home for girls aged 13-20 affected by sexual abuse and trafficking. The site frames this as tailored care plans and trauma-focused therapy to foster healing and future success.
- School Partnerships: Rosewill aims to collaborate with educational institutions to nurture and empower young women, offering essential care and skill-building as they transition into adulthood.
- Community Events: Rosewill aims to positively impact the community through events that enhance both the organization and its clients.
- Fundraisers: Annual fundraisers are presented as vital to supporting the mission and operational sustainability.
